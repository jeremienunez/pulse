# Brand asset recipe

Use the accepted brand direction, audience and content. Resolve the roles the assets must serve: a lead image, recurring mark, divider, texture, motion layer or a small family. Reuse the brand's established geometry, type roles, palette and material relationships where those already answer the need.

For an open identity, complete the shared cultural enquiry and root review before selecting an expressive direction. Turn retained branch outcomes into constraints on the family: density, rhythm, contrast, material character and the information that must remain readable.

Choose an admissible method for each needed role through the capability catalogue. Supply a permitted material image to image-based algorithms, or a documented reference and controls to an appropriate external authoring process. Keep the image role and output format explicit. Asset production is an admitted operation of the current task.

Generate only the variants needed to assess those roles. Record what is held constant and what each parameter changes. Preserve source/profile, seed when applicable, actual parameters, input/output hashes, licences and operation receipts. Do not claim seeded repeatability for an untested environment or a method using unbounded randomness.

Inspect the family at intended sizes and backgrounds, including small crops, narrow compositions and text overlap. Keep the selected assets and their relationship to the accepted direction in the existing evidence. A revision changes a named constraint and remains inside its budget. No extra brand state, catalogue import or automatic generator choice is created.
