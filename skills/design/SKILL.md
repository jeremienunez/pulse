# Design

Bring a web interface's purpose, visual direction and observed result together. Start through `pulse:using-pulse`; contribute to the current owner and its approved scope.

## Choose the contribution

Activation follows the intended effect on the interface, rather than a filename or framework. Backend work without UI impact and an exact copy correction need no design guide, research or variants.

Read only the guide needed now:

| Activity | Guide | Existing Pulse artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolve audience, content, purpose or open design choices | [Framing](references/framing.md) | Spec when needed, then `01_explore/output/decisions.md` |
| Build an approved interface or fix a local visual defect | [Implementation](references/implementation.md) | Current node and `04_execute/output/verification/` |
| Inspect and critique the result | [Review](references/review.md) | `05_review/output/review.json` and `uat.md` |
| Investigate an open expressive direction | [Cultural digging](references/cultural-digging.md) | Current research and its owner review |
| Specify UI motion or a film's temporal composition | [Motion](references/motion.md) | Current brief and verification |

Planning consumes the approved brief and its checks. Validation confirms the means needed to inspect them. An extension uses its established system; a new or replaced identity opens direction choices. Reuse accepted choices and ask only about material gaps.

## Ground the direction

Establish who visits, what they need to understand or do, the content's factual or illustrative status, and binding constraints. Choose the visitor mode per surface: `persuade` for deciding/acting, `operate` for completing a task, `read` for understanding, `experience` for encountering the work.

When references inform an open choice, inspect their relevant content or states. Record the observation, mechanism, limit and intended transformation before recommending a direction. A style name alone supplies no evidence.

For an open expressive direction, use the cultural method before recommendation or materialization. Develop at least two observation-induced research chains through cultural contexts, scenes, practices, works and processes, an inspected counterpoint, and decisions confirmed, changed or abandoned for this brief. The root reviews their substance separately from structural checks. Insufficient access, evidence or budget leaves the research partial.

For specialized asset production, load [visual-assets](../visual-assets/SKILL.md). Its capability contracts and local inspection establish the means of production; the cultural tree remains in the same task research.

Use existing project docs, tokens, components and assets as context. Task choices belong in the current decisions; no extra durable design document is required. Pulse retains approvals, revisions, execution strategy and finding severity.
