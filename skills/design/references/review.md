# Review the observed interface

Compare the current result with the approved brief and its acceptance checks. Use the existing root-owned Pulse review contract and artifacts.

## Read the experience, then the technical evidence

Inspect representative rendered sizes and states. Ask whether the audience can find the leading content/action, understand the groups, follow the path and recover from the changed flow. Assess hierarchy, density, type and rhythm against that surface's mode. Relate a visual choice to its approved reference transformation and content, rather than an aesthetic score.

Then audit the relevant mechanics: keyboard/focus, semantics, contrast, text expansion, responsiveness, motion and performance using the project's tools and targets. Preserve useful established behavior. Synthesize visual and technical observations after each has its own support; a clean mechanical check does not establish a coherent composition.

When the direction opened expressive choices, review the shared [cultural trace](cultural-digging.md) against the works actually inspected and the resulting transformations. Check the meaning of its qualified links and outcomes beyond the structural report. Reused approved research retains its identified scope. The root's substance findings and technical checks remain distinct.

## Keep the finding assessable

For each material issue, name the target, observed state, effect on the user's task, supporting capture/log/source, and a scoped remedy. Distinguish an observation from a mechanism hypothesis. A screenshot supports its displayed state; an interaction claim needs exercised states. Source review and unobserved runtime checks remain distinct.

Record findings using Pulse's `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, `open`/`closed` and existing schema. Severity follows impact. Put detailed observations and limitations in verification evidence and reference them from `review.json`/`uat.md`. Unavailable inspection remains unobserved with a concrete next check; it is not evidence of either correctness or a defect.

The review produces findings and UAT. Corrections use the existing recorded revision mechanism before returning to execution. Open P1 findings block Gate 4; other missing required evidence retains its actual limit for the human decision. Neither a design score nor this guide grants merge approval.
