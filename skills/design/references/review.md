# Review the observed interface

Read the observed result, then compare it with the approved brief and acceptance checks. Use the existing root-owned Pulse review contract and artifacts. A reviewer already exposed to the intention declares that limit; reading order alone cannot make the author independent.

## Read the experience, then the technical evidence

Inspect representative rendered sizes and states. Ask whether the audience can find the leading content/action, understand the groups, follow the path and recover from the changed flow. Assess hierarchy, density, type and rhythm against that surface's mode. Form a reading of the idea from actual evidence before confronting it with the approved reference transformation and content. For a selected expressive quality bar, use the full [jury method](jury.md) and [rubric](awwwards-quality-bar.md): preserve its detailed criteria, budgets, proof per score and concrete ranked fixes. A number alone is not the review.

Then audit the relevant mechanics: keyboard/focus, semantics, contrast, text expansion, responsiveness, motion and performance using the project's tools and targets. Preserve useful established behavior. Synthesize visual and technical observations after each has its own support; a clean mechanical check does not establish a coherent composition.

When the direction opened expressive choices, review the shared [cultural trace](cultural-digging.md) against the works actually inspected and the resulting transformations. Check the meaning of its qualified links and outcomes beyond the structural report. Reused approved research retains its identified scope. The root's substance findings and technical checks remain distinct.

## Prepare an honest visual critique

Use [jury](jury.md) to establish target, production build, evidence coverage and scorecard. Read every supplied frame/state and verify that it is the actual requested page. A missing render remains unobserved. The four weighted criteria and six developer areas retain their precise descriptions; their ambition is not reduced to generic consistency. [Quality](awwwards-quality.md) supplies known bugs and the distinction between HTML-server and rendered-DOM evidence, load/loader/settled times, hover signals and real interaction.

When an independent critic is authorised, give a fresh one only the [quality-bar critic brief](awwwards-quality-bar.md#critic-brief-for-an-authorised-fresh-independent-critic-on-each-scoring-pass), rubric and actual screenshots/measurements. Do not give this guide, the source, approved concept, earlier score or calibration. First collect the perceived idea, then compare it with the accepted brief. Otherwise label the actual self-review and its limits. The [historical calibration](jury-calibration.md), including its two cases and old bug, belongs only to the card author and is not automatically applied to a new reviewer/model. Scores and findings support the existing Pulse decision; they do not grant a gate.

## Keep the finding assessable

For each material issue, name the target, observed state, effect on the user's task, supporting capture/log/source, and a scoped remedy. Distinguish an observation from a mechanism hypothesis. A screenshot supports its displayed state; an interaction claim needs exercised states. Source review and unobserved runtime checks remain distinct.

Record findings using Pulse's `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, `open`/`closed` and existing schema. Severity follows impact. Put detailed observations and limitations in verification evidence and reference them from `review.json`/`uat.md`. Unavailable inspection remains unobserved with a concrete next check; it is not evidence of either correctness or a defect.

The review produces findings and UAT. Corrections use the existing recorded revision mechanism before returning to execution. Open P1 findings block Gate 4; other missing required evidence retains its actual limit for the human decision. Neither a design score nor this guide grants merge approval.
