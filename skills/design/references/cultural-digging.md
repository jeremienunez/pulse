# Cultural digging

Use this method before recommending or materializing an open expressive direction. Begin with the audience, content, outcome and binding constraints already accepted for the task. Reuse identified, relevant research when the direction remains approved. Mechanical reproduction and backend work retain their scope.

## Ask a question that can change the project

Name the unresolved relationship: how a scene communicates belonging, how a practice organizes attention, or how a material process could express this content. State what an answer could confirm, change or make you abandon. Start with several discovery routes, then let inspected evidence redirect them.

An archive, index or search result supplies leads. Open the actual work, project, screen or flow and preserve its inspected support, origin, scope, observation and anchor. A blocked source stays blocked/non-observed; its refusal proves access limits. SVA Archives and index.opensource are discovery routes, with access and image permissions assessed separately.

## Branch, inspect, rebound

Develop a tree connecting cultural context, scenes/subcultures, genres/practices, works and processes. These are local roles, not a universal cultural taxonomy or a required historical sequence. Add cross-links and abandoned branches when the evidence calls for them.

From an inspected work, identify a concrete discovery and the new question it raises. Seal that question and its triggering reference/support before searching. Preserve the search result, then inspect a work it actually led to. Repeat this observation-induced sequence in at least two distinct lead branches. Two preplanned queries or duplicate branches do not establish these chains.

Use both proximity and distance: a peer may expose an inherited convention, a contemporary work a different organization, and a cultural source the practice's context. A discovery can send you sideways, back to an earlier question, or into another medium. Explain that move rather than arranging unrelated references beneath attractive labels.

Qualify each relationship:

| Relation | Required basis |
| --- | --- |
| Documented influence | A source supporting that historical influence |
| Observed affinity | Inspected forms or behavior, with the shared relationship named |
| Shared technique | Evidence of the actual process or construction |
| Documented opposition | A source supporting the declared disagreement |
| Hypothesis | An explicit unresolved proposition and its limit |

An affinity does not establish a historical influence. A retained transformation needs positive inspected support beyond a hypothesis.

## Examine a counterpoint and decide

Inspect a distinct work that challenges a lead branch. Name the assumption it tests and whether the comparison changes, narrows or rejects that branch. For each required branch, record the outcome for this brief: confirmed, changed or discarded; before/after; target need; reason. Keep unresolved and abandoned branches visible.

Derive a concrete transformation from retained branches: hierarchy, rhythm, reading path, type relationship, material treatment or interaction. An anti-perfect departure needs a reason and a readable boundary. A cultural label does not directly choose a style or asset generator.

## Preserve the trace and review its substance

Keep references, supports, queries, results and outcomes in the task's existing research. In creative sessions 1.1, `cultural_tree` resolves those existing identities; rebounds are bound to admitted request/receipt order, and directions name their retained branch and inspected reference. Follow the [creative protocol](../../../docs/evaluation/frontend-creative.md) for exact fields and versioning.

The root reads the works, relations, questions and consequences, then binds its substance findings to the research digest. A structural PASS only establishes the checked structure and provenance. A source list, a decorative tree or a root declaration without its supports cannot replace the enquiry.

Research does not finish at a search count. Continue while a decision-relevant gap can be examined within the approved budget; stop partial when evidence, access or time cannot support the recommendation. The two-chain minimum is a trace requirement, not a quality score. Never invent an inspection, filiation or completed rebound to close the task.
