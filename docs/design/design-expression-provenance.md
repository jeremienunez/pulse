# Design expression and visual assets — provenance

This candidate separates design/motion guidance from specialized asset production. `design` owns the shared cultural method; `visual-assets` selects a method against effect, input role, output, controls, rights and inspected availability. Pulse retains the task artifacts, approvals, budget and review. No retrieval service, external lifecycle or media provider is introduced.

## Cultural enquiry

An open expressive choice develops inspected works through cultural contexts, scenes/subcultures, practices, works and processes. Two actual observation-induced research chains, a counterpoint and consequences for the brief precede recommendation/materialization. Tree links distinguish documented influence, observed affinity, shared technique, documented opposition and hypothesis. The root reviews the substance independently of structural validation.

Anti-perfect expression is a situated choice of rhythm, asymmetry or material irregularity. It is not an automatically applied style. Covered extensions reuse identified relevant research; backend and exact-copy work retain their scope.

[SVA archives](https://archives.sva.edu/) and [index.opensource](https://indexopensource.com/index) are discovery sources. Their discovery proves neither work inspection nor image permission. No SVA artwork was inspected or used in this pilot. The actual pilot separates historical context from contemporary procedural affinity and does not invent direct influence.
