## Reproduction and maintenance

Use an approved Pulse scope, the exact frozen corpus, explicit control/candidate conditions and the existing evaluator. Preserve unavailable metadata, failed operations and actual supplied reference bytes; do not replay uncertain effects or make passing cases harsher to justify an intervention. Keep responses and proofs in the canonical feature history, not in runtime guidance.

For a changed condition, record the baseline and retest under `pulse:writing-pulse-skills`, obtain applicable Pulse approvals, and verify source generation, links, word budgets and an independently extracted package. Structure/package success does not certify model behavior. The source integration, installation, commit and push retain their own authorization.
